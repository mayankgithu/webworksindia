[01] The brief

What needed solving.

Parents needed a dependable way to find a verified tutor near home, and tutors needed a steady flow of genuine tuition leads. Doing it over phone calls and spreadsheets didn't scale, so the business needed one platform for parents, tutors and the office team.

[02] What we built

Features, end to end.

01

Smart tutor finder

Parents filter by subject, class, board and locality, and book a free demo in one form.

02

Requirement posting

Parents post what they need; the office reviews it before it goes live to tutors.

03

Tutor onboarding and KYC

Tutors sign up, upload documents and get approved before they can apply.

04

Admin assignment

Jobs are shared out across the office team so every lead has an owner.

05

Live tuition-job feed

Approved requirements stream to tutors across the site.

06

Attendance and affiliates

Class attendance tracking and a referral program for partners.

07

Installable web app

Parents and tutors can add it to their home screen like an app.

08

Search pages

Pages for every subject, class, board and Lucknow locality, built for Google.
