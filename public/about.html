About the studio

A tech studio from Lucknow, building for the whole internet.

Web Works India designs and builds websites, online stores, custom software and CRMs, and helps businesses get found on Google. One team does all of it, so your website, software and WhatsApp work together from day one.

We don't sell templates or vanity rankings. We build systems that bring enquiries, show you the work every week, and hand you everything we build.

[02] How we work

From first call to launch day.

Five stages, each with something you can see and approve.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We learn your business, customers and goals, and tell you honestly what will and won't help.

    Day 1
  2. 02

    Proposal and plan

    A written scope, fixed price or milestones, timeline and the exact list of what you'll receive.

    2–3 days
  3. 03

    Design

    You see real screens of your project before a line of code is written, and approve them.

    1–2 weeks
  4. 04

    Build and review
